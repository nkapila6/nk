It's an interesting day... while we all thought life was coming to normal until yesterday evening... Boom! We get hit by so many mobile alerts and all the woowee mood goes down the drain.. work goes back to home.. Is this the new normal?

Anyway, have been talking to and looking at alternatives for the future. What should I do, how to proceed? Life is just too confusing right now. Under normal circumstances if it was a job or project, I would just quote Alan Watts: "Muddy waters are best cleared by leaving it alone".. the only problem with this situation is that: does it make sense to take the risk by waiting (leaving it alone)?

Right now, there seems to be no panic of sorts but it's weird times we live in with high risk.. Do world leaders not feel pressure or think twice when making such decisions? Why can things not be settled by speaking? Why the need to ego flex your muskles to the world? I don't have the answers to these questions but I wish life was much more simpler than it is right now.

While it may seem like I'm panicking, I really am not but in these confusing times where you have built your life around a place for so many donkey years, it's natural to feel concerned.

## Amidst uncertainty, upskill continues

In the meantime, I still am using this time to distract myself into continuous upskilling and learning new stuff. I'm not really learning new AI stuff but upserting old and more foundational knowledge which may seem counterintuitive right now but my intuition tells me it will definitely help me more than all the AI stuff I know.

- React: yes, you heard it right React! As much as I can just vibe code front-ends, I'd rather love to build a few myself so I can even vibe effectively if I have to. :)
- CMU DB course: Things are moving on this front. I know a lot more about databases than I did before.
- VPS stuff: Playing around deploying stuff on my very own VPS!! This is really good exposure.
- Algorithms: Does it help in my work? No! Will it help in my work? Maybe! Do I feel it's important? 100 percent!!
