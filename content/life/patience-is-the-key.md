Life has wonderful ways to teach you things.

Facing many adventures & rollercoasters, it has definitely ignited in me that good & warm-hearted people are very much out there.
My visit to Finland was so warm that I think I changed as a person when I came back home.
I now have another family I feel proud of and it has boosted my confidence levels through the roof in many different aspects which I'm not really able to articulate. Maybe I articulate this in the next post?

The most important thing I learnt is patience. Patience in work, patience in relations, especially patience in situations which helps a lot to revive your lost perspective due to possible anger or frustration.

The revival of perspective really helps you to imagine yourself in the other persons shoes and understand where he/she might react in a certain way and not judge him/her.

I think that's a huge feat all due to a simple thing like patience.
Really helps to reignite the focus that was being clouded as well.

Thank you for this important lesson, Finland!
