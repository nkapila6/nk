Logic, Superiority v/s Emotional Abdication...........

<center><img src='https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nkapila6/nk/refs/heads/v4/content/life/resources/human-psyche.jpg' /></center>

The fact that our psyche constantly warns us at different points in time that something is going wrong with the current power dynamic being faced and how that power dynamic can prove to be harmful in the future thru intuitive extrapolation is actually amazing and really intriguing.
Facing such situations again reminds you of the PAST "ill-power dynamics" faced...
It is indeed an amazing AUTOMATIC feedback system.

The human's need to have a higher status-quo / higher power dynamic to show dominance, superiority is funny and I suppose "happy-hormone" inducing.

It's there from our childhood. Which 10yr old kid doesn't like to look cool?
I used to admire our best football player and how the crowd cheered him/her from the stands.
Don't we all?

I think it's more to do with the fact of being implicit in nature through achievements rather than showing someone something.. However, this does not apply to HR / relationships or where direct human social contact maybe required.

There's a vicious cycle to be maintained especially when being with illiterate people.
If you give them too much by being too grateful, they take you for granted.
If you give them too less, they serve you very well.
But then, for some people at least me(less on an average), the 2nd line really tackles my moral programming as I tend to go into the extremes of emotional abdication.

\*I would like to take my label of illiterate back as it applies to most educated people too.

The sweet sugary see-saw balance required can be achieved by moving in between the markers of being grateful and also being able to get good done work by abdication of emotion.

It is something I personally find very tough to do since I tend to go into extremes of emotion abdication as I don't like to be a bureaucratic leader but someone who leads through high hopes and collective decision making.

Maybe it's my over exaggerated nature of going to into the extremes or maybe... it's something acquired through experience..? (Better known as Social Engineering).

Well, only the UNBROKEN power dynamic of time will tell me..
